# Glossary

The abbreviations used in the guidelines, with their meaning and the guideline where they matter most. On the [website](https://depuschm.github.io/architecture-guidelines/), each of them shows its meaning when you hover over it. Everyday developer terms, such as API, CI/CD, HTTP, JSON, SQL or UI, aren't listed.

When a guideline introduces an abbreviation that readers may not know, add a row here, in alphabetical order. Keep the meaning to one plain sentence without links or formatting, because the website uses it as the tooltip text.
