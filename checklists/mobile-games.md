# Mobile games (iOS and Android) checklist

Copy this list into a pull request, design review or project kickoff and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Automatable* names tools that can check the rule in CI (see the file's "How to enforce" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

This file is generated by `python3 scripts/rank.py fix` from the guidelines. Don't edit it by hand.

## Strongly recommended (9–10)

- [ ] `MOBGAME-1` (9) Verify every purchase on your own server before granting it, and keep entitlements and currency balances there, not on the device. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-5` (9) Treat each store's build requirements as release blockers with deadlines: Google Play's target API level and Play Billing Library version, and the App Store's minimum Xcode and SDK version. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md)) *Automatable: Android Lint target SDK checks, App Store Connect upload checks.*
- [ ] `MOBGAME-6` (9) Declare what the game and every SDK in it collect: keep the App Store privacy details and privacy manifests, and Google Play's Data safety form, accurate, and ask for permission through App Tracking Transparency before tracking players on iOS. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md)) *Automatable: App Store Connect upload checks.*

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `MOBGAME-2` (8) Complete a purchase only after granting it: acknowledge or consume Google Play purchases from your backend, and finish App Store transactions after the content is delivered. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-3` (8) Process the stores' server notifications on your backend, Real-time developer notifications from Google Play and App Store Server Notifications V2, to keep subscriptions current and to claw back refunded or voided purchases. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-7` (8) Decide the target audience before choosing ad, analytics and social SDKs, and answer both stores' age-rating questionnaires accurately. If children are part of the audience, follow Google Play's Families policies and the App Store's rules for kids' apps. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-8` (8) Run live operations, such as events, offers and balance changes, from remote configuration and downloadable data with safe in-app defaults, not from downloaded code. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-9` (8) Track user-perceived crash and ANR rates for every release, and keep them under Android vitals' bad-behavior thresholds: 1.09% and 0.47% overall, and 8% on any phone model. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md)) *Automatable: Play Console pre-launch report.*
- [ ] `MOBGAME-4` (7) Show the odds of each item type before a player buys randomised items such as loot boxes. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-10` (7) Keep the first download small: ship what the first session needs in the base app, and deliver the rest as asset packs with Play Asset Delivery and Background Assets. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-11` (7) Protect ranked play, trading, rewards and other economic features by checking app and device integrity on your server, with the Play Integrity API and App Attest. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-12` (7) Release updates gradually, with staged rollouts on Google Play and phased release on the App Store, and halt or pause the rollout when crash or ANR rates rise. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
- [ ] `MOBGAME-13` (7) Cap the frame rate at what the game needs, and lower it and the rendering load when the device heats up, before the system throttles the game. ([specific/mobile-games](../specific/mobile-games/README.md))
