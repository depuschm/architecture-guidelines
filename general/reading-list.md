# Reading list

Complete tutorials, guides and books, referenced as a whole. Topic files link to specific chapters where relevant.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or strongly recommended, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or conditional.

## Free online guides and tutorials

- `9` [Software Architecture Guide](https://martinfowler.com/architecture/) (Martin Fowler): a curated set of articles on architecture.
- `9` [Azure Architecture Center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/guide/architecture-styles/): styles, patterns, best practices and reference architectures.
- `9` [AWS Builders' Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/): articles on how Amazon builds and runs distributed systems.
- `9` [Google SRE books](https://sre.google/books/): *Site Reliability Engineering*, *The Site Reliability Workbook* and *Building Secure and Reliable Systems*.
- `8` [Software Engineering at Google](https://abseil.io/resources/swe-book): engineering practices at scale.
- `8` [The System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer): a structured self-study course on large-scale system design.
- `8` [Microservice Architecture](https://microservices.io/patterns/index.html) (Chris Richardson): a pattern language with examples.
- `8` [Game Programming Patterns](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/contents.html) (Robert Nystrom): design patterns for games and real-time software, free to read online.
- `6` [The Architecture of Open Source Applications](https://aosabook.org/en/): real systems explained by their authors.
- `6` [Refactoring.Guru](https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns): an illustrated design patterns tutorial.
- `6` [Patterns.dev](https://www.patterns.dev/): design, rendering and performance patterns for web apps.
- `5` [Domain-Driven Design Quickly](https://www.infoq.com/minibooks/domain-driven-design-quickly/): a free introductory e-book.
- `5` [Awesome Software Architecture](https://github.com/mehdihadeli/awesome-software-architecture): a large curated link collection.
