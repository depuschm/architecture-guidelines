## Key rules

### Renderer and pipeline compilation

- `7` `GODOT-16` **Choose the renderer for the platforms and oldest hardware you ship to: Forward+ for desktop games that need advanced rendering features, Mobile for mobile devices and standalone XR, and Compatibility for the web, older devices or the widest hardware support.** Set the `rendering/renderer/rendering_method.mobile` and `.web` overrides when platforms need different renderers, and decide early: switching later means adjusting scenes, lighting and environments, most of all between Compatibility and the other two renderers. This applies `GFX-1`. ([Godot, Overview of renderers](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/renderers.html), [ProjectSettings, rendering_method.mobile](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_projectsettings.html#class-projectsettings-property-rendering-renderer-rendering-method-mobile))
- `7` `GODOT-17` **In Forward+ and Mobile, let pipeline precompilation see everything during loading: show a simple scene that uses every rendering feature the game needs, such as MSAA, reflection probes or VoxelGI, before loading most assets, and instance effects that scripts add during play once as hidden nodes.** Watch the debugger's pipeline compilation monitors for compilations outside loading screens. In Compatibility, show each material, shader and particle effect for at least one frame while loading instead. This applies `GFX-5`. ([Godot, Reducing stutter from shader (pipeline) compilations](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/pipeline_compilations.html), [Performance, pipeline compilation monitors](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_performance.html#class-performance-constant-pipeline-compilations-draw))

### Materials, instancing and level of detail

- `7` `GODOT-18` **Reuse materials, and where you can't, reuse shaders: share one material across objects and give each object its own values through per-instance uniforms (`instance uniform`), not a unique material.** Put values that many shaders read, such as wind or the player's position, in global shader uniforms set with `RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_set()`, and don't read them back every frame, which makes the calling thread wait for the rendering thread. This applies `GFX-10`. ([Godot, GPU optimization: reuse shaders and materials](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/gpu_optimization.html#reuse-shaders-and-materials), [Shading language: per-instance uniforms](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/shading_language.html#per-instance-uniforms), [global uniforms](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/shading_language.html#global-uniforms))
- `6` `GODOT-19` **Draw thousands of identical objects, such as grass, flocks or particles, with a MultiMesh, and split it into several MultiMeshes by area, because Godot culls a MultiMesh only as a whole.** In Forward+, opaque or alpha-tested MeshInstance3D nodes that share a mesh and material are instanced automatically. This applies `GFX-10`. ([Godot, Optimizing 3D performance: instancing](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/optimizing_3d_performance.html#use-manual-instancing-multimesh), [Optimization using MultiMeshes](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/using_multimesh.html))
- `6` `GODOT-20` **Keep the mesh LODs that Godot generates on import, and add visibility ranges (HLOD) where a group of objects should turn into one simpler mesh or an impostor in the distance, or where distant effects should disappear.** The two work together, even on the same mesh. This applies `GFX-14`. ([Godot, Mesh level of detail (LOD)](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/mesh_lod.html), [Visibility ranges (HLOD)](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/visibility_ranges.html), [Optimizing 3D performance: level of detail](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/optimizing_3d_performance.html#level-of-detail-lod))
